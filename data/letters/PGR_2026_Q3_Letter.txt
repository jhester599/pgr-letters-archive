Letter to Shareholders
Third Quarter 2026
In the extremely competitive third-quarter marketplace we maintained growth, though at a more moderate pace than in prior periods. Our net premiums written (NPW) grew 4% when compared with the third quarter of 2025, in part reflecting our rate reductions as we compete in this soft market. Our year-over-year policies in force (PIF) growth moderated slightly to 6%, but unit growth was still strong given our large base of customers. Of course we want units to increase, so we continue to create plans for growth in both our Personal Lines and Commercial Lines segments and will spend on media to spur growth as long as that spend remains efficient. All that said, as market growth turned slightly negative, our performance relative to peers remained strong, underscoring the resilience of our business. Our companywide margins continued to be strong, with the combined ratio (CR) during the quarter at 88.7 and 87.5 year to date (YTD).
Personal Lines – personal auto, special lines, and personal property – delivered strong profitability relative to seasonal targets during the third quarter. NPW of $19.9 billion increased 4% while the CR of 88.0 improved by 1.5 points, compared to the same quarter last year. Though new business applications were down slightly against third quarter 2025, renewal applications increased by 9% and PIFs grew by 6% during the period.
We continue to invest aggressively across channels to obtain new customers and retain our existing ones. Quarterly media spend was up 18% over last year, driving new business volume at elevated but still efficient customer acquisition costs, and we continue to invest in agency growth incentives across most states and agencies. On the non-acquisition expense side, we remain focused on expense discipline and reduced our Personal Lines vehicle non-acquisition expense ratio by 0.4 points YTD versus the prior year period, through operating efficiency gains.
During the quarter, our personal vehicle business NPW increased 4%, while PIFs rose 7%, at a better than target 88.7 CR. We continue to benefit from the enhanced segmentation and risk selection of our latest personal auto product model (9.0), which was in-market in 21 states that represented 55% of personal auto PIFs and 53% of trailing 12-month personal auto NPW at quarter end.
In personal property, we continued to prioritize profitability and portfolio quality by focusing on lower-risk properties, reducing exposure in catastrophe-prone markets, and encouraging bundled homeowners business. These actions contributed to a CR of 72.8 for the quarter and 76.3 year to date. As the health of our property insurance business has improved, we have begun shifting our focus toward growth opportunities in markets where we believe we can confidently achieve our target returns. New business applications in our homeowners product (which excludes our renters and umbrella products) increased 22%, compared to the third quarter last year.
Speaking of personal property, we are excited about the progress our dedicated personal property claims organization is making to create competitive advantage, as we apply the playbook that has worked so well in personal auto, special lines, and Commercial Lines, where we’re #1.
Our success in claims has been grounded in our Guiding Principles for more than twenty years. It’s a balance of Accuracy, Efficiency, Customer Experience, and Employee Engagement that has served us extremely well. Leveraging the combination of Progressive’s enterprise strengths and evolving property expertise, we are building a property claims organization with outstanding leadership, forward-thinking technology solutions, and a stellar training experience for which Progressive is known.

I often talk about the investments we make in people, processes, technology, and data, and that certainly applies here. Since the end of 2017, we have increased our dedicated personal property staff more than fivefold, bringing adjusting functions in-house that we previously outsourced. We are also about to open a state-of-the-art training facility in Arizona. This is a purpose-built, immersive, learning environment designed to bridge the gap between classroom instruction and real-world field application. This hands-on component of the training journey enables learners to apply inspection, estimating, and customer-service skills in a realistic residential setting before ever working with customers.
Unlike traditional training environments, the facility incorporates real damaged materials and construction components sourced from contractors and restoration partners. These items provide exposure to actual fire, water, smoke, and other property damage conditions, giving learners hands-on experience identifying and evaluating real-world losses. Technology is integrated throughout the environment to support modern claims processes, including audiovisual capabilities, scenario-based learning, estimating exercises, and opportunities to practice vendor coordination and mobile device workflows.

Our Commercial Lines (CL) business finished the third quarter with NPW growth of 9% at a 92.6 CR. Core commercial auto continued to be a significant contributor to NPW growth in the quarter, driven by an increase in both new and renewal applications, which we attribute to targeted rate decreases in states and business market targets in which we have margin and increased investment in advertising and agent incentives. New business application growth was driven by both increased quoting, particularly in our trucking products, and improved conversion.
Based on data from S&P Global Market Intelligence, the commercial auto industry, excluding Progressive, finished the first half of 2026 with an estimated CR of approximately 104, highlighting continued profitability challenges across the market. In response, competitors continued taking rate and underwriting actions. Industry data, including second quarter 2026 survey results from the Council of Insurance Agents & Brokers, shows commercial auto rate increases continue in the mid-single digits. We believe our competitive position will continue to strengthen as competitors raise rates and tighten underwriting. We are also optimistic that increased shopping activity in the market, reflected in the higher level of quoting we are seeing, will continue to support new business growth.
We continue to invest in our products and segmentation and, in the third quarter, we launched our new 8.4 core auto product model in two states. This new product adds meaningful segmentation capabilities that we believe drive profitable growth, while expanding our appetite to include trucking segments we have not previously served. We expect to elevate our 8.4 model in five additional states in the fourth quarter, with all seven states representing 15% of our countrywide trailing 12-month core commercial auto NPW as of the end of the third quarter 2026.
We launched our business owners’ policy (BOP) product in California during the third quarter, bringing our total state rollout to 47 states. California, with over four million small businesses, is the largest small business insurance market in the country and represents a significant growth opportunity.
